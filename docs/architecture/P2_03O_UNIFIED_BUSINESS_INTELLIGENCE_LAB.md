# P2-03o — Unified Business Intelligence Lab

## Delivery scope

Source-only, free, non-operational **integrated demonstrator**. Twelve interconnected views share P2-03m reconciled sales and identified-cost evidence and P2-03j Growth Autopilot decisions. The browser reads the checked-in contract generated by `scripts/build_intelligence_lab_contract.py` from strictly bounded synthetic fixtures; not from actual business accounts.

### Twelve modules, working demonstration functions

| Module | Verified scope of demo |
|---|---|
| Objective Workspace | Selected business goal and illustrative contribution target gap |
| Business Value Intelligence | Equal-period financial trends and six-category known-cost waterfall |
| Decision Intelligence | Bounded, transparent +/-50% price / volume / unit-cost sensitivities |
| Growth Autopilot | P2-03j review / defer / suppress decisions, zero live dispatch |
| CRM Intelligence | Consent-coverage, opt-out and followup aggregate counts only |
| Inventory Intelligence | Review flags for reorder thresholds and illustrative stock value |
| Financial Intelligence | Known costs against sample budget; cash and profit explicitly unavailable |
| Marketing Intelligence | Engagement and review queue; attribution explicitly unavailable |
| Sales & Commerce Intelligence | Completed orders and commerce activity; no invented conversion funnel |
| Workflow Automation | Human-managed, in-memory browser plan; never sends or executes |
| Operations & Workforce Intelligence | Open, completed and overdue example work |
| Executive Intelligence | Joined scorecard with evidence-needed recommendations |

### Five shared intelligence engines

1. Measurement — calculations reconcile to prior source-engine inputs.
2. Decision — pure arithmetic hypothetical sensitivity, explicitly not a forecast.
3. Recommendation — deterministic rules: target gaps, stock, consent, backlog, content review. Priorities mean *review urgency in example*, not evidence of revenue increment.
4. Automation — no real dispatch; every review task remains browser-local and cannot perform side effects.
5. Learning — two modeled 30-day period deltas; there is **no verified causal uplift**.

### User interface

New review-only path: `apps/site/intelligence-lab/index.html`. White-first responsive layout, original brand mark, landmark / semantic navigation, labels and focus indicators, native inputs and source-integrity checks, mobile layout at 1440 / 390 / 360 CSS pixel widths. All user adjustments and task statuses vanish upon reload. No sign-in, payment, localStorage, cookie, analytics, requests to external services or collected contact details. This page is not linked from the public homepage and is **not deployed**.
