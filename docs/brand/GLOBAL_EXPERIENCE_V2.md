# MetaAutomate Global Experience v2 — Design and Product Standard

## North star

Build a distinctive global technology-company experience. International standards come from precise interfaces, honest technical claims, durable engineering, accessibility, coherent narrative and a useful path from problem to solution. Do not imitate big-tech logos, invent customer figures or present an early-stage product as a launched SaaS.

## Signature design language

- Identity: approved MetaAutomate blue/silver/gold globe-infinity mark; no substitute logo.
- Colors: Midnight Navy #040D20, Deep Navy #071932, Electric Blue #00A7ED, Silver #DCE6F1, Signature Gold #EFB94F.
- Visual direction: high-contrast editorial typography, precise engineering grid, dimensional signal graphics, technical instrument panels, deliberate whitespace, bold section cadence, premium responsive composition.
- Motion: no unnecessary animation, no dependency on motion for meaning, full reduced-motion support.
- Performance: no network fonts, analytics, frameworks, or external image libraries.
- Accessibility: semantic HTML, visible focus rings, live regions for brief copying, keyboard-operable system tabs, mobile nav with ARIA-expanded and Escape dismissal.

## Information architecture

1. Home: “Build beyond the expected.” Capability architecture, systems thinking, service disciplines, focus sectors and actual development-stage Objective Workspace proof.
2. Services: Six interdependent disciplines—AI Systems & Automation, Software & Digital Products, Cybersecurity & Quality, Cloud & DevOps, Data & Decision Intelligence, Product Design & Experience.
3. Company: Mission, vision, original brand symbolism, Integrity/Excellence/Innovation/Impact/Always HALAL, strategic roadmap.
4. Engage: A real project discovery interaction that validates inputs and creates shareable text without pretending to submit a lead or save personal information.

## Truth and commercial readiness

The website offers engagement categories and meaningful examples, not guaranteed resources, engagement dates, pricing or proven project outcomes. Do not invent a client logo, corporate registration, city of operation, certification, 24/7 support, global office, revenue or business email.

Before activating live lead submission, verify a company-owned contact channel and provide a privacy notice appropriate to the data being collected. Do not secretly transmit project briefs to third parties.

MetaAutomate's autonomous capability execution remains blocked behind its own security and authorized-execution milestone. Human approval of a plan is not permission to run it.

## Engineering acceptance

Check 1440px desktop, 390px and 360px mobile in Chromium. Test all page routes, anchored services, status text, navigation, system tabs and arrow-key switching, company values, field validation, TXT download, reset and no outbound browser requests. Check Render for static-only publishing and no secrets, and inspect screenshots before public rollout.

The public static site is separate from private FastAPI and PostgreSQL. Automatic Render deployment stays off until owner approval.
