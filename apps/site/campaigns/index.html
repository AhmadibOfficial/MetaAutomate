01 / INVENTORY DATA

Work with your own stock records.

CSV: sku,name,stock,weekly_sales,unit_price_kobo. Optionally add stock_count_status,sales_coverage_status in that order. Stock status: counted/unknown; sales coverage: complete/partial/unknown. For dated records, additionally append stock_observed_on,sales_period_start,sales_period_end as strict UTC calendar dates (YYYY-MM-DD). A dated weekly-sales period must cover exactly seven calendar dates, and stock/sales data must be at most seven days old; future and stale dates are held. Dated fields and statuses are your own declarations, not independent verification. Incomplete enhanced rows are held for review; older five- and seven-column CSVs remain usable with visible warnings. Prices are integer Nigerian kobo (₦1 = 100 kobo). Maximum 400 items and 128 KB. Do not include personal or confidential information in this public-browser prototype.

Processed in your browser. No upload request.
